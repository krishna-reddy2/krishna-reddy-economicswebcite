Institute Information

Technical Training Institute of Pune Blind Men's Association provides different types of courses on a 3.5-acre campus with quality hostel facilities and dining for both males and females.

TTI of PBMA offers IT sector courses such as artificial intelligence, SQL, web designing, and web accessibility training.

Our aim is to empower visually challenged students to succeed in IT and corporate interviews.
