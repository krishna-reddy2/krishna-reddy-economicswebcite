meaning of demand

In the ordinary language demand means desire. Suppose a person desires to have a car it is called demand in ordinary language.

The quantity of a commodity purchased by a consumer at a particular price, at a particular time in a given market is called demand.

determinents of demand

Demand for one commodity is influenced by several factors. All these factors are called determinants of demand.

  • price of the product
  • income of the consumer
  • prices of the related goods
  • tastes and habbits of the consumers
  • advertisement affect
  • unknown variable or natural facts
