Economic Growth and Development

Course Overview

Economic Growth and Development is a course that provides an in-depth analysis of the factors that influence economic growth and development.

Course Objectives

  • Analyze the concepts and measures of economic growth and development.
  • Evaluate the theoretical frameworks of economic growth, including classical, neoclassical, and endogenous growth models.
  • Examine the role of institutions, governance, and policy in promoting economic growth and development.
  • Assess the impact of globalization, trade, and foreign investment on economic growth and development.
  • Apply empirical evidence and case studies to understand the experiences of different countries and regions.

Course Topics

  1. Introduction to Economic Growth and Development: Concepts, measures, and trends in economic growth and development.
  2. Theories of Economic Growth: Classical, neoclassical, and endogenous growth models.
  3. Institutions and Governance: Role of institutions, governance, and policy in promoting economic growth and development.
  4. Globalization and Trade: Impact of globalization, trade, and foreign investment on economic growth and development.
  5. Economic Development and Poverty Reduction: Strategies for promoting economic development and reducing poverty.
  6. Case Studies: Experiences of different countries and regions, including East Asia, Latin America, and Africa.
  7. Policy Debates: Current policy debates and challenges in promoting economic growth and development.

Learning Outcomes

  • Theoretical frameworks of economic growth and development.
  • Role of institutions, governance, and policy in promoting economic growth and development.
  • Impact of globalization, trade, and foreign investment on economic growth and development.
  • Strategies for promoting economic development and reducing poverty.
  • Empirical evidence and case studies of different countries and regions.

Assessment

  • Quizzes and exams.
  • Homework assignments and problem sets.
  • Group projects and presentations.
  • Final research paper or project.

Prerequisites

  • Principles of Macroeconomics.
  • Intermediate Macroeconomics.
  • Mathematical Economics.

Target Audience

  • Graduate students in economics, business, or related fields.
  • Advanced undergraduate students in economics, business, or related fields.
  • Professionals seeking to understand economic growth and development.
